Modularity

Any sampler runs with any executor. Pick random, quasi-random, Gaussian-process or successive-halving samplers, and run them serially, on threads or on a cluster.

Benchmarked samplers

The samplers are benchmarked and, where possible, compared with the literature to check that their performance matches, with practical recommendations on which to pick.

Convenience

Checkpointing, clean stopping, time limits, failure tracking, stateful objectives and summary plots.

Quick example ​

julia
using BigHOPs, CairoMakie, AlgebraOfGraphics

f(x; pre_artefact=nothing) = ((x.a - 1)^2 + (log10(x.lr) + 2)^2 + 1 / x.r, nothing)
domains = (a=Continuous(-5, 5), lr=Continuous(-4, -1; transform=exp10))
ho = Hyperoptimizer(Stateful(f), domains, BOHB(R=27))
run!(ho; executor=Threaded())
printmin(ho)
fig = summaryplot(ho; figure=(; size=(1200, 600), fontsize=24),
                  axis=(; xticklabelsize=24, yticklabelsize=24))
